# Source Manifest & Lawful Provenance

## Provenance Summary
This project utilizes **100% synthetically generated industrial telemetry and safety event data**, engineered in strict conformance with international automation and industrial safety standards. No proprietary, confidential, personal, or restricted third-party data was harvested, ingested, or redistributed.
